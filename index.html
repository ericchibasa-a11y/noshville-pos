Operating Expenses

Rent, salaries, electricity and maintenance. Cash means paid from the till. Record equipment in Assets and owner withdrawals in Cash / Bank Movements.

Asset Register

Asset numbers are assigned automatically when saved. Record equipment such as fridges, shelving and POS devices. A paid purchase records its funding movement automatically; do not also enter it in Expenses.

Post Depreciation

Enter a reviewed depreciation amount. Posting reduces net book value and charges depreciation expense in the ledger; it does not pay cash.

Cash / Bank Movements

Record payments and funds already moved. Asset purchases are recorded in Assets. Stock payments here only record the funding; receive stock in Stock / Purchases.

Enter till movements before cash-up. Corrections use a reversal on an open date and keep the original record.

Bank Statement & Reconciliation

One Noshville business bank account. EFT sales and bank expenses/movements post to its bank book. Card sales stay in Card Settlement Clearing until the actual settlement is recorded. Importing a statement does not create income or an expense.

Set up bank opening balance

Use the balance immediately before the first included bank transaction. The opening date must be on or before the first recorded bank transaction.

Capture a statement line

Headings: Date (YYYY-MM-DD), Description, Debit, Credit; optional Reference and Balance. The same file cannot be imported twice.

Record bank book transactions

Use Expenses, Assets or Cash / Bank Movements for those transactions. For card settlements and bank-only adjustments, use the balanced Journal form in Accounting.

Match statement to bank book

Select the actual corresponding entry. Matching requires equal amounts and direction; it does not post another transaction.

Accounting & Financial Reporting

Manager ledger, trial balance, draft financial statements and month-end controls, following Feasterville’s structure. Retail cost of sales uses the costs saved on each sale. Stock receipts debit inventory and credit suppliers; record their actual payments once in Cash / Bank Movements.

Draft management reports. Opening till/inventory, creditors, debtors, loans, capital and historic payments must be captured and reconciled. Tax is shown only when posted by your accountant; no automatic tax calculation is applied. This is the same provisional basis used by Feasterville.
